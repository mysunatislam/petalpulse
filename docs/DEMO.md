# Demo walkthrough

A 3–4 minute path through the seven demo moments. On the welcome screen, tap **Explore the demo**. Everything you see is synthetic data for a fictional user, Mira, and a banner says so on every screen.

| # | Moment | What to tap | What to point out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mira opens the app and sees her cycle overview | (Home opens) | The Petal Compass in soft rose for cycle day 2, the next period as a **range** with a confidence level, "How is this estimated?" |
| 2 | She records strong cramps on the body map | My Care → **Log pain** → tap the lower abdomen and lower back → Continue → choose 7 → Continue → Cramping → **Save & see comfort ideas** | Words beside every number, the list alternative to the map, and the gentle "worth an appointment" note at 7+. Then the Comfort Path. |
| 3 | Petal AI responds with care | **Petal AI** tab → "My stomach hurts so much." | It asks where and how strong, mentions her own pattern as a pattern (not a cause), and offers the pain map, comfort ideas and "When should I see a doctor?". Optional: type a crisis message to show that safety comes first and nothing is sent to the cloud. |
| 4 | She logs feeling emotionally exhausted | Home → **How is your heart feeling today?** → Exhausted | One tap, no form. |
| 5 | The app shows a personalised self-care plan | **A gentle plan for today** → answer two quick questions → **Make my gentle plan** | Small, optional steps, no streaks, "Talk it through with Petal". |
| 6 | Pattern Garden shows a meaningful trend | **Insights** tab | E.g. cramps on the first two days of bleeding, or feeling irritated before her period, each with "How did we find this?" and correlation-not-causation wording. |
| 7 | She exports a health summary for a clinician | My Care → **Pain Passport** → **Export a summary for my clinician (PDF)** → **Create PDF** | A clean one-page summary, watermarked as demo data. |

To leave the demo: Settings → **Leave demo mode**, which removes all demo data.

Tips for a live demo:

- Use a real Android device with a development build so haptics, the encrypted database and PDF sharing all work.
- Turn on dark mode on the phone to show the night theme.
- Switch the language to বাংলা in Settings to show Bangla onboarding and replies (detail screens are still partly English).
